# SCPI & Paper Real Estate: Institutional Diversification for Hands-Off Investors

Investing in Civil Real Estate Investment Companies (*Sociétés Civiles de Placement Immobilier*) delivers European property exposure starting from just €1,000.

---

## 1. Institutional Asset Diversity
SCPI funds pool hundreds of millions of euros to acquire prime European healthcare facilities, corporate headquarters, and cross-border logistics hubs.

## 2. Key Advantages
- **Zero Landlord Responsibilities**: The management company handles tenant leasing, unpaid rent recovery, and building maintenance.
- **Geographic Diversification**: Spread risk across France, Germany, the Netherlands, and Spain.
