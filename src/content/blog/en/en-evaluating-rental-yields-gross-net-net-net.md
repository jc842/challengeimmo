# Decoding Rental Yields: The Difference Between Gross, Net, and Net-Net ROI

Advertising brochures frequently boast high "Gross Yields" that disguise the true economic return of an investment property.

---

## 1. Gross Yield Formula
$$\text{Gross Yield} = \frac{\text{Annual Rent}}{\text{Total Purchase Price}} \times 100$$
While easy to calculate, it neglects every real-world operating expenditure.

## 2. True Net-Net Yield (*Rentabilité Nette-Nette*)
Deducting building service charges (*charges de copropriété*), property insurance, property tax (*taxe foncière*), management agency fees (typically 6%–8%), and personalized income taxes reveals the actual cash return in your bank account.
