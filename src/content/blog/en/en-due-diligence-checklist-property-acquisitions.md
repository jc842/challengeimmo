# The Master Due Diligence Checklist: Technical Diagnostics & Legal Verifications

Before removing conditions precedent (*levée des conditions suspensives*), an investor must thoroughly audit every legal, technical, and financial aspect of the property.

---

## 1. Co-Ownership Minutes (*Procès-Verbaux d'AG*)
Reviewing the last three general assembly minutes reveals upcoming capital expenditure assessments, roof repairs, elevator renovations, or unresolved tenant disputes.

## 2. Technical Energy Diagnostics (DPE)
Buildings rated F or G (*passoires thermiques*) face escalating rental bans across France unless comprehensive thermal insulation and window upgrades are completed.
